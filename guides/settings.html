Settings Reference

Tap Settings in the bottom bar to open Preferences. Engine Control is at the top. Below it, the settings are grouped into pages. This page explains each one.

🖼️ Screenshot placeholder: settings/01-preferences.png

The Preferences page with Engine Control and the list of settings pages.

Engine Control

The status of the live wallpaper.

ItemWhat it does
Service Running / Service Not RunningShows whether Scrolla is your live wallpaper now. When it runs, it also shows the active playlist, the current wallpaper and the network state.
Set WallpaperOpens the Android live wallpaper preview, so you can set Scrolla as your wallpaper.
StopStops the wallpaper service. Your phone goes back to the system default wallpaper.
Restart EngineRestarts the wallpaper engine. Try this first when the wallpaper looks wrong or does not move.
Refresh Status & TelemetryReads the current network and playback state again.

Wi-Fi name not shown?

On Android 10 and newer, the app can only read the Wi-Fi name when Location (GPS) is on in Quick Settings and the app has Precise location permission.

Playback

Playlists

Multiple Playlists Strategy: what happens when more than one playlist is active.

OptionResult
Flatten & MergeAll wallpapers from the active playlists play as one big playlist. The settings of each playlist are not used.
Round-robinOne wallpaper from each active playlist, in turn.
SequentialAll wallpapers of one playlist, then all wallpapers of the next playlist.

Default Playlist Settings: switch interval, shuffle, and switch on lock or unlock, for all playlists. See Playlists.

Wallpapers

  • Default Wallpaper Settings: Screen Fit, Base Color, Pre-Transform, Animations, Audio and 3D settings for all wallpapers. See Wallpaper Settings.
  • Scene: layers, clock, audio visualizer and shapes around your wallpapers, for the profile you are editing. See Scenes.
  • Loading Wallpaper and Fallback Wallpaper: what shows while wallpapers are being prepared, and when no wallpaper is available. See Wallpaper Settings.

Rendering

Frame Rate Limit: a lower frame rate uses less battery and makes the phone less warm.

  • 15 FPS — Battery saver
  • 30 FPS — Balanced
  • 60 FPS — Standard
  • Custom (10 to 120)

Preloading

  • Preload Next Wallpapers: prepare the next 0 to 3 wallpapers in advance, so switches are smoother. More uses more memory.
  • Enable Video Preloading: also prepare videos in advance. This can cause stutter or battery drain on slower phones. The app limits it to 1 video.

Cloud & Storage

Cloud

ItemWhat it does
Cloud Storage ManagerConnect and manage Google Drive and Custom Repositories. See Cloud Storage.
Backup & RestoreCloud and ZIP backups. See Backup & Restore.

Transfers

ItemWhat it does
Max Concurrent Cloud TransfersHow many cloud uploads or downloads run at the same time.
Cloud Transfer NetworkAny Network (Wi-Fi or mobile data) or Wi-Fi Only.
When Wi-Fi Is LostOnly with Wi-Fi Only. Pause stops running downloads and resumes them later. Continue lets them finish.
Unrestrict network for Dynamic Playlist BatchesOnly with Wi-Fi Only. Dynamic Playlist batches download on any connection, including mobile data.

Cache

  • Cloud Cache Persistence Limit: the maximum space for cloud thumbnails and downloads (100 MB to 10 GB).
  • Background Cache Eviction: deletes old cached cloud wallpapers every N days (0 to 30). Set it to Never to keep them.

Misc

Only for signed-in users. Maximize Compatibility (experimental) changes how Custom Repositories work. Use it at your own risk.

Performance

Thumbnails

  • Thumbnails Generation: Unified (minimal storage) uses less space. Differ by Frame Size (best performance) loads faster. Changing it clears the thumbnail caches.
  • Animated Thumbnails: show moving thumbnails for animated wallpapers. To apply a change to existing thumbnails, tap Clear Thumbnail Caches.
  • Clear Thumbnail Caches: deletes all thumbnails. The app makes them again when needed.

Processing

Max Concurrent Processes: how many thumbnails the app makes at the same time in the background. Lower it if the app is slow while you scroll through a big library.

Appearance & Content

Appearance

  • Theme: the colors of the app, per profile. See App Themes.
  • Home Screen Widget: colors, opacity and wallpaper tags of the widget. See Home Screen Widget.

Content

NSFW Content Visibility: how wallpapers with the NSFW tag show in the app: Hidden, Blur or Show. See Content Safety Tag.

Privacy & Permissions

ItemWhat it does
Ad Privacy OptionsChange your ad consent choices. Shown only where the law asks for it.
Non-personalized AdsAds are not based on your interests. Ads still show. Always on, and locked, while Telemetry is Off.
TelemetryWhat the app sends to the developer when something goes wrong: Default, Minimal or Off. See Telemetry levels.
Permissions ManagerAllow or check Alarms & Reminders, Location Access (Precise) and Microphone Access. See First Launch Setup.

Your wallpapers, playlists, tags and settings are never sent.

  • A lower level applies at once.
  • A higher level applies after you restart the app. The app tells you so.

Advanced

ItemWhat it does
Wallpaper Engine LogsShows service events, wallpaper switches and errors. Logs are deleted after 3 days.
Show Debug InfoShows technical information on the wallpaper. It can slow down the wallpaper.
Export DatabaseSaves a copy of the app database to your Downloads folder. The developer can ask for it to find a bug.
Restore Default SettingsResets all settings to their defaults. Your wallpapers, playlists and tasks are not deleted.

The App Version is at the bottom of the Preferences page. Include it when you report a bug.

Account

To sign in, tap Sign in with Google at the top of the Preferences page. When you are signed in, tap your picture to open your User Profile, with View Purchase History and Sign out.
