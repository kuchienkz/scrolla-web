# Delete Your Account and Data

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

This page explains how to delete your **Scrolla Wallpaper** account and the data that belongs to it. The App is made by **Kuchienkz**. You only have an account if you signed in with Google in the App; without one, there is nothing on my server to delete.

## Delete it in the App

1. Open **Settings** and tap your profile picture at the top right.
2. Under **Privacy**, tap **Delete account**.
3. Read the summary and tap **Delete account**.
4. Pick the Google account you signed in with. This confirms that it is you.

The account is deleted at once. My server then deletes its data, usually within a few minutes.

## Ask me by email

If you no longer have the App, or it does not work for you:

1. Email **wahyupamengas.developer@gmail.com** from the address of your Scrolla account.
2. Use the subject **"Delete my account"**.

I delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply when it is done. I may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. I never ask for your password.

## What is deleted

- Your account (Firebase Authentication) and your profile: account ID, email address, display name and profile photo link.
- Your Pro status, your purchase history, and the links between your purchases and your account.
- The account key of your encrypted backups. Backups locked to your account **cannot be opened** after this. To keep a backup, make a new one with a password first.
- Your bug reports, feature suggestions and translation reports, with their status.
- A free Pro gift, if you had one.

## What is not deleted

- **Your Scrolla Pro subscription.** Deleting the account does not cancel it. Cancel it in **Google Play** > **Payments & subscriptions** first, or Google Play keeps charging you.
- **Data on your phone:** wallpapers, playlists, tasks, scenes and settings stay until you delete them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.
- **Your Google Drive:** files the App put in your Drive stay there. You can remove the App's access in your [Google Account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- **Google Play order records:** Google keeps them under its own policies and as tax and accounting law requires.
- **Crash and error reports:** they hold no account data and are deleted automatically by Sentry after a limited time.

See the [Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md) for what the App collects and why.
