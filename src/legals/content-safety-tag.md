# Content Safety Tag

Scrolla Wallpaper uses the tag **NSFW** ("not safe for work") to mark wallpapers that contain adult or sensitive content.

## Why you should use it

The app can hide or blur NSFW wallpapers in its screens, for example in the library, playlist covers and previews. This helps when other people can see your screen.

::: info
The NSFW tag only changes how wallpapers show **inside the app**. It does not stop a wallpaper from playing as your live wallpaper. To keep NSFW wallpapers off your screen, do not add them to active playlists, or add **NSFW** to the **Excluded Tags** of your [Dynamic Playlists](../guides/dynamic-playlists.md).
:::

## Mark a wallpaper as NSFW

1. Open the wallpaper editor. It opens after an import. For an existing wallpaper, long-press it in the library and tap **Configure**.
2. Tap **Edit Tags**.
3. Search for `NSFW` and add it.
4. Tap **Save**.

If you are not sure whether a wallpaper is safe, mark it as NSFW.

Wallpapers in your cloud repository keep this mark, so it also works on your other devices.

Pictures that you download from a [Custom Repository](../guides/custom-repositories.md) get the NSFW tag automatically when the website does not rate them for all ages.

## Choose how NSFW wallpapers show

Go to **Settings** > **Appearance & Content** > **NSFW Content Visibility**:

| Option | Result |
|---|---|
| **Hidden** (default) | NSFW wallpapers are not shown. |
| **Blur** | NSFW wallpapers are blurred. |
| **Show** | NSFW wallpapers are shown normally. While ads are shown in the app, they are blurred. |
