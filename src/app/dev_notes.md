# Dev Notes

## **2026/10/23: 3.0 Beta**

Version 3.0 is out as a Beta on Google Play. Some users may not have access to it yet. It is the biggest update since 2.0, so expect a few rough edges. The [guides](../guides/README.md) follow this version, and the [changelog](../changelogs.md) lists every change.

### Highlights

- **Scenes**: put a clock, an audio visualizer and shapes on top of your wallpaper, and edit them live in the Scene Editor. See [Scenes](../guides/scenes.md).
- **Themes**: color the app your way, per profile, with a second theme for dark mode. See [App Themes](../guides/themes.md).
- **Home Screen Widget** and a **Next wallpaper** Quick Settings tile. See [Home Screen Widget](../guides/home-screen-widget.md).
- **Custom Repositories**: search and download from public wallpaper APIs. See [Custom Repositories](../guides/custom-repositories.md).
- **Video sound**, with volume and fade. See [Wallpaper Settings](../guides/wallpaper-settings.md#audio).
- **Better backups**: they now include subscribed cloud wallpapers (as links), scenes, themes and Custom Repositories. See [Backup & Restore](../guides/backup-restore.md).
- **Privacy choices**: pick a **Telemetry** level (**Default**, **Minimal** or **Off**) and turn on **Non-personalized Ads**. See [Telemetry levels](../guides/settings.md#telemetry).
- **Settings** are now grouped into pages, so things are easier to find. See [Settings Reference](../guides/settings.md).

## **2024/06/8: 2.0 Beta**

After 2 years of development (mostly procrastination lol), finally version 2.0 released into the wild. Again, it's still in BETA, meaning there are bugs lurking in the dark waiting to be found. Expect the unexpected.
