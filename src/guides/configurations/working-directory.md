# Working Directory

The Working Directory is the folder where Scrolla Wallpaper keeps:

- your **wallpaper files** (the copies made when you import),
- the **database** with your playlists, tasks, tags and settings,
- other app data.

## Select the Working Directory

You select it in the [Setup Wizard](../first-launch-setup.md#working-directory), on the **Setup Working Directory** step. The list under **App-Specific Directory Options** shows the folders that your phone offers to Scrolla:

- the app's folder on **internal storage**,
- the app's folder on **external storage**, for example an SD card, if your phone has one.

In the list, `~/` means your phone's main storage (`/storage/emulated/0/`).

::: tip Which one should I select?
Select the internal storage folder if you are not sure. Select the SD card folder if your phone has little internal storage and you plan to import many large wallpapers or videos.
:::

## Important: uninstalling deletes this folder

These folders belong to the app. They are private, so other apps and your gallery cannot see the wallpapers in them. But **Android deletes them when you uninstall Scrolla Wallpaper.**

Before you uninstall the app or reset your phone, make a backup. See [Backup & Restore](../backup-restore.md).

## Free up space

Imported wallpapers use space in the Working Directory. To use less space:

- delete wallpapers you do not use,
- move big collections to [Cloud Storage](../cloud-storage.md) and keep only the ones you need on your device.
