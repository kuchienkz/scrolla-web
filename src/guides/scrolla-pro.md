# Scrolla Pro

Scrolla is free. **Scrolla Pro** is an optional subscription for people who want more. Everything else, the audio visualizer included, stays free.

## What Pro includes

| | Guest (not signed in) | Signed in (free) | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Playlists** | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Tasks** | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Dynamic playlists** | 1 | 1 | Unlimited |
| **Cloud Drive repositories** (Google Drive) | 1 | 1 | Unlimited |
| **Custom API repositories** | 1 | 1 | Unlimited |
| **Ads** | Yes | Yes | No ads |

- A dynamic playlist also counts as a playlist. The count covers all profiles (Home and Lock Screen).
- Signing in is free and already gives you more playlist and task slots. Open your user profile and sign in with Google.

## During the beta {#beta}

While Scrolla is in beta, everyone gets **unlimited playlists, tasks and repositories** for free. Ads stay on. The Pro page and your user profile show **Free during the beta**.

You can still subscribe to Pro during the beta. Pro then removes the ads. When the stable version comes, the free beta slots end, and your Pro stays.

## Free Pro offers {#free-pro-offers}

From time to time, Scrolla gives free Pro for a limited time: to everyone (a **promotion**), or to one account (a **gift**).

- Free Pro gives **unlimited playlists, tasks and repositories**. Ads stay on.
- The Pro page shows the offer and when it ends. Your user profile shows **Free Pro promotion** or **Pro gift**.
- The offer starts and ends on its own. You do not need to update the app.
- When the offer ends, items above your free limit are [locked](#locked-items). Nothing is deleted.
- To remove the ads during an offer, subscribe to Pro.

## Get Pro

1. Sign in with your Google account. Pro belongs to your **Scrolla account**, not to the phone.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Scrolla Pro**. You can also tap **Remove ads** under the banner, or **Get Pro** when you reach a slot limit.
3. Choose **Yearly** or **Monthly**. The page shows the price in your currency and how much you save with Yearly.
4. Tap **Subscribe** (or **Start free trial** when a trial is offered to you) and finish the payment in Google Play.

Google Play offers the payment methods of your country, for example cards, carrier billing, e-wallets, UPI, gift cards and cash payments.

::: tip Payment pending
With a cash payment or some e-wallets, Google Play waits for the payment. The app shows **Payment pending**. Pro starts as soon as Google Play confirms the payment. If you do not pay, nothing is charged.
:::

## Pro on every device

Sign in with the same Scrolla account on another phone, and Pro is there. The app remembers your Pro status, so it also works offline.

A purchase stays with the account that bought it. If you are signed in to another account, the app says **This purchase belongs to another Scrolla account** and shows a hint of the right email address.

## Restore purchases

New phone or reinstalled app? Sign in with the same account. Pro comes back on its own. If it does not, go to **Settings** > **Scrolla Pro** and tap **Restore purchases**.

## Change, cancel or renew

- **Change plan:** on the Pro page, pick the other plan and tap **Subscribe**. Google Play replaces your current plan and counts the time you already paid for.
- **Cancel:** tap **Manage subscription** on the Pro page, or open Google Play > **Payments & subscriptions** > **Subscriptions**. Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Renewal:** the subscription renews automatically until you cancel. A free trial becomes a paid yearly subscription unless you cancel before it ends.
- **Payment problem:** if a renewal fails, Google Play retries for a few days and Pro stays on. If it keeps failing, Pro stops until the payment is fixed.

## No ads until midnight

Not ready for Pro? Tap **Watch ad: no ads until midnight** (under the banner, or on the Pro page) and watch one short ad to the end. The app then shows no ads until midnight on your phone. It works without signing in, once a day.

If you close the ad before the end, there is no reward.

## Locked items

When you have more playlists, tasks or repositories than your plan allows, the newest ones above the limit are **locked**. This happens when you sign out, when Pro ends, or when you restore a backup with many items.

A locked item shows a **Locked** badge. Nothing is deleted:

- you can **view**, **export** and **delete** a locked item,
- you cannot edit, activate, duplicate or run it,
- the wallpaper never plays a locked playlist, and a locked task never runs,
- a locked Cloud Drive repository does not upload, download, sync or index, and you can still remove it or refresh its login. Wallpapers you already downloaded from it stay on your device,
- a locked Custom API repository does not show in the browser, and you can still remove it.

Your oldest items stay usable, up to the limit. To unlock items, sign in, get Pro, or delete other items. They unlock on their own, with their settings and their on/off state as you left them.

## Questions

**Does Pro unlock other features?** No. Pro removes ads and the slot limits. All other features are free for everyone.

**I have free Pro but I still see ads.** Free Pro (the beta, a promotion or a gift) keeps the ads. Only a Pro subscription removes them.

**I paid but I still see ads.** Check that you are signed in with the account you bought Pro with, and that the phone was online at least once since. Then tap **Restore purchases**. If it still does not work, email **wahyupamengas.developer@gmail.com**.

**Refunds** follow [Google Play's refund policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).
