# Home Screen Widget & Quick Settings

Control the live wallpaper without opening the app: with the **Scrolla Playlist** widget on your home screen, or with the **Next wallpaper** tile in Quick Settings.

## Add the widget

1. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen.
2. Tap **Widgets**. The name depends on your launcher.
3. Find **Scrolla Wallpaper**, then drag the **Scrolla Playlist** widget to your home screen.
4. Resize it if you want. A taller widget also shows wallpaper tags (see below).

## What the widget shows

- The **active playlist** and the **current wallpaper** of your home screen.
- A **countdown** to the next wallpaper switch. A `~` means an estimate (for example when the switch comes after a number of scroll cycles).
- Optional: **wallpaper tags**, one tag category at a time.

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Next wallpaper** | Switches to the next wallpaper now. |
| **Automatic switching on / off** | Pauses or resumes the automatic switch (interval, unlock, cycles). **Next wallpaper** still works. |
| **Play / pause animation** | Freezes or resumes the scroll animation. |
| **Save wallpaper to Downloads** | Saves a copy of the current wallpaper file to your Downloads folder. |
| **Override audio setting on / off**, **volume down / up** | For video wallpapers with sound: turn the sound on or off and change its volume, whatever the wallpaper's [audio settings](wallpaper-settings.md#audio) say. These buttons are faded when the wallpaper is not a video. |

On a phone with a mouse or stylus, hold the pointer over a button to see its name.

## Change the widget's look

Go to **Settings** > **Appearance & Content** > **Home Screen Widget**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Background opacity** / **Background color** | The widget background. |
| **Text opacity** / **Text color** | The text and the buttons. |
| **Reset colors and opacity** | Back to the default look. |
| **Show Wallpaper Tags** | Shows tags of the current wallpaper below the buttons. The widget must be at least about 150 dp tall. |
| **Tags per category** | How many tags of each category to show. |
| **Tag categories** | Which [tag categories](tags-and-datasets.md) to show. Drag them to set the order. Select at least one. |

The tags slide up every few seconds, one category at a time.

## Quick Settings tile

1. Swipe down twice from the top of the screen to open Quick Settings.
2. Tap the **edit** (pencil) button.
3. Drag the **Next wallpaper** tile into your tiles.

Tap the tile to switch to the next wallpaper.
