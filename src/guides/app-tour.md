# App Tour

This page shows where to find things in Scrolla Wallpaper.

## Bottom bar

The bottom bar has five buttons:

| Button | What you find there |
|---|---|
| **Playlists** | Your playlists. Turn playlists on and off, and start the live wallpaper. |
| **Tasks** | Automation rules that change wallpapers or playlists for you. |
| **Resources** | Your wallpaper library and your tags. Import new wallpapers here. |
| **Settings** | Wallpaper service controls, default settings, performance, cloud and backup. |
| **Profile** | Opens the profile panel from the right side. Select which Home Screen or Lock Screen profile you are editing. |

## Playlists tab

- **Top-left wallpaper icon**: opens the Android live wallpaper preview, so you can set Scrolla as your wallpaper.
- **Title**: shows "Playlists" and the name of the profile you are editing.
- **View button**: switches between **Big List View** and **Folders View**.
- **+ button**: creates a **Standard Playlist** or a **Dynamic Playlist**.
- **Playlist card**: tap to open it. Use the switch to turn it on or off. Long-press to select several playlists.

More: [Playlists](playlists.md).

## Tasks tab

- **New Task**: creates an automation task.
- **Execution Logs**: shows what your tasks did, and why.
- **Validate tasks**: checks your tasks for problems.
- **Big List View / Folders View**: changes the layout.

More: [Tasks](tasks.md).

## Resources tab

The **Resource Manager** has:

- **WALLPAPERS** card: opens your full wallpaper library.
- **TAGS** card: opens the list of all tags.
- **Latest Wallpapers**: tap to show or hide your most recent imports.
- **Dataset button** (top-right): manages tag datasets.
- **Menu button** (bottom-right): **Add Wallpapers** and **Add Wallpapers + Metadata**.

More: [Importing Wallpapers](importing-wallpapers.md), [Managing Your Wallpaper Library](managing-wallpapers.md), [Tags & Datasets](tags-and-datasets.md).

## Settings tab

Settings are grouped into sections: **Engine Control**, **System & Integration**, **Playback Behavior**, **Performance & Caching**, **Appearance**, **Content Preferences** and **Advanced**.

More: [Settings Reference](settings.md).

## Profile panel

Tap **Profile** in the bottom bar to open the panel. It lists your profiles under **Home Screen** and **Lock Screen**. The profile marked **Active** is the one you are editing now.

From the panel you can also open:

- **Configure Profiles**: create, edit, turn on and delete profiles.
- **Transfer & Migration History**: cloud upload and download history.

More: [Profiles](profiles.md), [Cloud Storage](cloud-storage.md#transfer-history-and-settings).

## Words used in this guide

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Wallpaper** | An image or video that you imported into Scrolla, together with its tags and settings. |
| **Playlist** | A list of wallpapers that play one after another, like a music playlist. |
| **Active playlist** | A playlist that is turned on. The live wallpaper plays wallpapers from active playlists. |
| **Profile** | A separate set of playlists, tasks and folders for the Home Screen or the Lock Screen. |
| **Task** | An automation rule: "when this happens, and these conditions are true, do this". |
| **Wallpaper service** / **Engine** | The part of the app that draws the live wallpaper on your screen. |
