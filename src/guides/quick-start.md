# Quick Start

This guide takes you from a fresh install to a scrolling live wallpaper. It takes about five minutes.

You will do four things:

1. Finish the Setup Wizard.
2. Import some wallpapers.
3. Put them in a playlist and turn the playlist on.
4. Set Scrolla Wallpaper as your live wallpaper.

::: tip Why "Scrolla"?
Most wallpaper apps crop your image to fit the screen. Scrolla Wallpaper does not crop. It slowly **scrolls** across the full image instead, so wide panoramas and tall artworks stay complete.
:::

## Step 1: Finish the Setup Wizard

The first time you open the app, the **Setup Wizard** starts.

1. Tap **Get Started**.
2. Grant the permissions you want, or skip them. They are optional. See [First Launch Setup](first-launch-setup.md#permissions) for what each one does.
3. Select a **Working Directory**. The default option is fine for most people.
4. Read and accept the Terms and Conditions, the Privacy Policy and the Background Location statement.
5. Review the summary, then finish the wizard. You can skip account registration.

::: details 🖼️ Screenshot placeholder: `quick-start/01-setup-wizard-welcome.png`
The "Welcome to Scrolla Wallpaper" screen with the **Get Started** button.
:::

## Step 2: Import wallpapers

1. Tap **Resources** in the bottom bar.
2. Tap the round menu button in the bottom-right corner.
3. Tap **Add Wallpapers**.
4. Select one or more images or videos, then confirm.
5. If the app asks **Check Duplicates?**, tap the option you prefer. Checking takes a little longer but keeps your library clean.
6. The **New Wallpaper(s)** editor opens. You can add tags here, or leave everything as it is.
7. Tap **Save**.

::: details 🖼️ Screenshot placeholder: `quick-start/02-add-wallpapers-menu.png`
The Resources tab with the bottom-right menu open, showing **Add Wallpapers** and **Add Wallpapers + Metadata**.
:::

Scrolla Wallpaper copies each file into its own storage. You can delete the original files later if you want.

::: tip Mark sensitive images
If an image is not safe for work, add the tag **NSFW** in the editor. The app then hides or blurs it in previews. See [Content Safety Tag](../legals/content-safety-tag.md).
:::

## Step 3: Create a playlist and turn it on

1. Tap **Playlists** in the bottom bar.
2. Tap the **+** button at the top, then tap **Standard Playlist**.
3. Enter a name, for example "Favorites", then tap **Create**.
4. Tap the new playlist to open it.
5. Tap **Add wallpapers** at the bottom.
6. Tap the wallpapers you want to add. Then tap **CONFIRM (SELECT WALLPAPERS)** at the bottom.
7. Go back to the Playlists tab.
8. Turn on the **switch** on the playlist card.

::: details 🖼️ Screenshot placeholder: `quick-start/03-playlist-switch.png`
The Playlists tab with one playlist card. Highlight the on/off switch on the card.
:::

::: warning
An empty playlist cannot be turned on. Its card shows **Tap to add wallpapers** instead of a switch.
:::

## Step 4: Set Scrolla Wallpaper as your live wallpaper

1. On the **Playlists** tab, tap the **wallpaper icon** in the top-left corner.
2. Android opens its live wallpaper preview. You should see your wallpaper scrolling.
3. Tap the apply button (the label depends on your phone, for example **Set wallpaper**).
4. If your phone asks, select **Home screen** or **Home screen and lock screen**.

::: details 🖼️ Screenshot placeholder: `quick-start/04-android-live-wallpaper-preview.png`
The Android live wallpaper preview with the apply button visible.
:::

You can also start the wallpaper from **Settings** > **Engine Control** > **Set Wallpaper**.

## Done!

Go to your home screen. Your wallpaper now scrolls slowly across the full image.

## What next?

- Change how fast and in which direction it scrolls: [Animations](animations.md).
- Fix the zoom or position of one wallpaper: [Wallpaper Settings](wallpaper-settings.md#pre-transform).
- Change wallpapers every few minutes or on unlock: [Playlists](playlists.md#playlist-settings).
- Use a different setup on the lock screen: [Profiles](profiles.md).
- Switch playlists automatically at night or at home: [Tasks](tasks.md).
