# App Themes

A theme sets the colors of the app, and can put a picture behind its pages. Each [profile](profiles.md) has its own theme, so you always see which profile you are editing.

Go to **Settings** > **Appearance & Content** > **Theme**.

::: details 🖼️ Screenshot placeholder: `themes/01-theme-list.png`
The Theme page with built-in and custom themes, and the "Follow system dark mode" switch.
:::

## Pick a theme

The list shows **Built-in** themes and your **Custom** themes. Tap a theme to use it for the profile that you selected in the profile panel.

New installations use a light theme for the **Home** profile and a dark theme for the **Locked** profile.

## Follow the system dark mode

Turn on **Follow system dark mode** to use a second theme while your phone is in dark mode. Then select the **Theme in dark mode**.

## Make your own theme

Tap **New theme**, or open the menu of a theme and tap **Duplicate and edit**. Built-in themes cannot be changed, but you can duplicate them.

The **Preview** at the top shows real buttons, panels and text with your colors.

### Basics

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name of the theme. |
| **Light** / **Dark** | A light or a dark theme. |
| **Seed color** | The main color. Every color that you do not set yourself comes from it. |
| **Seed from a wallpaper** | Takes the seed color from a library wallpaper. |
| **Style** | How the other colors are made from the seed, for example **Tonal (default)**, **Vibrant** or **Monochrome**. |
| **Contrast** | **Normal**, **More** or **High**. |

### Background

| Type | What shows behind the pages |
|---|---|
| **Solid color** | The background color of the theme. |
| **Picture** | A picture from your phone. Tap **Choose picture**. |
| **Library wallpaper** | A wallpaper from your library. Tap **Choose wallpaper**. |
| **Current wallpaper** | The wallpaper that plays now. The live wallpaper must be running. |

With a picture, set **Blur**, **Dim** and **Panel opacity**. The app dims the picture as much as needed to keep text readable.

### Colors

Each color group is **From seed** or **Custom**. For a custom color you can also pick the **Text and icon color**, or keep **Automatic (always readable)**.

::: tip Always readable
The app checks every text color against its background. If a color you picked is hard to read, the app adjusts it a little and says **adjusted for readability**.
:::

Tap **Save** when you are done.

## Edit or delete a theme

Open the menu of a custom theme and tap **Edit** or **Delete**. Profiles that used a deleted theme go back to their default theme.

Custom themes are part of your [backups](backup-restore.md).
