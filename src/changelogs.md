# Changelogs

## **Version 3.0 (Beta)**

The 3.0 Beta is available on Google Play to Beta testers. Some users may not have access to it yet. The [guides](guides/README.md) follow this version.

### New

- **Scenes**: text, an audio visualizer and shapes on top of your wallpaper, edited in a live Scene Editor. Wallpapers can have their own scene. See [Scenes](guides/scenes.md).
- **Text and variables**: any text in your own font, size, color, shadow and outline, with live values such as the time, the date, the battery level, the volume and the network. The digital clock is now a text component; your clocks keep their look. See [Text and variables](guides/scenes.md#text) and [Variables](guides/variables.md).
- **Presets**: add a ready-made **Clock**, **Clock and date** or **Battery**, or save a layer as your own preset. See [Presets](guides/scenes.md#presets).
- **Replace groups**: a wallpaper's own components can hide the profile scene's components of the same group, for example its own clock instead of the profile clock. See [Replace groups](guides/scenes.md#replace-groups).
- **Scrolla Pro**: an optional subscription without ads and with unlimited playlists and tasks. During the beta, playlists and tasks are unlimited for everyone. See [Scrolla Pro](guides/scrolla-pro.md).
- **Themes**: built-in and custom app themes per profile, a second theme for dark mode, and pictures or your wallpaper behind the pages. See [App Themes](guides/themes.md).
- **Home Screen Widget** and a **Next wallpaper** Quick Settings tile. See [Home Screen Widget](guides/home-screen-widget.md).
- **Custom Repositories**: search and download from public wallpaper APIs. See [Custom Repositories](guides/custom-repositories.md).
- **Audio sync calibration**: measure the sound delay of each output (phone speaker, Bluetooth headphones) so the visualizer bars match the sound. See [Calibrate audio sync](guides/scenes.md#calibrate-audio-sync).
- **Video sound**: video wallpapers can play their audio, with volume and fade. See [Wallpaper Settings](guides/wallpaper-settings.md#audio).
- **Loading and Fallback wallpapers**: choose what shows while wallpapers load, or when none is available.
- **Cloud wallpapers** bring their settings and scene, and you can keep your own version on one device.
- **Backups** now include subscribed cloud wallpapers (as links), scenes, themes and Custom Repositories, with backup options and a warning for drives that are not connected.
- Smoother drag, pinch and panorama movement on the live wallpaper.
- **Privacy choices**: a **Telemetry** setting (**Default**, **Minimal** or **Off**) and a **Non-personalized Ads** switch, in the first-launch setup and in **Settings** > **Privacy & Permissions**. See [Telemetry levels](guides/settings.md#telemetry).
- Settings are now grouped into pages: **Playback**, **Cloud & Storage**, **Performance**, **Appearance & Content**, **Privacy & Permissions** and **Advanced**. See [Settings Reference](guides/settings.md).
