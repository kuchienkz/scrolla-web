About

Say goodbye to cropping wallpapers!

Why crop a wallpaper if you can SCROLL it? Scrolla Wallpaper is a live wallpaper app. It slowly scrolls across any image, of any size, so the full picture stays visible. You control the direction, speed and animation.

New here? Start with the Quick Start.

Features

SMOOTH SCROLLING AND ANIMATIONS

Scroll images in any direction. Add enter and exit animations such as fade, slide and bounce. Use automatic rules to give wide, tall or video wallpapers their own animations. Learn more

IMAGES, VIDEOS AND 360° PANORAMAS

Use images, animated GIFs, videos and 3D panoramas with gyroscope and touch control. Video wallpapers can play their sound. Learn more

SCENES: CLOCK, AUDIO VISUALIZER AND SHAPES

Add a digital clock, music-reactive bars and shapes on top of your wallpaper. Give a single wallpaper its own scene that comes and goes with it. Learn more

PLAYLISTS

"Play" wallpapers with playlists, like a music player's playlist. Shuffle them, change them every few minutes, or change them when you unlock your phone. Dynamic Playlists fill themselves from your tags. Learn more

HOME AND LOCK SCREEN PROFILES

Use a different setup on your home screen and your lock screen. Learn more

TASKS

Change wallpapers or switch playlists automatically, based on time, day, Wi-Fi network, battery and more. Learn more

HOME SCREEN WIDGET

Skip to the next wallpaper, pause switching or the animation, and save the current wallpaper, right from your home screen or Quick Settings. Learn more

THEMES

Color the app your way, per profile, with a picture or your wallpaper behind the pages. Learn more

WALLPAPER MANAGEMENT

Import with tags from metadata files, find duplicates, search and filter by tags. Learn more

GOOGLE DRIVE AND BACKUP

Keep big collections in your Google Drive and use them on all your devices. Search public wallpaper APIs with Custom Repositories. Back up your setup to the cloud or to a ZIP file. Learn more
